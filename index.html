EST. 1961 · INDIANAPOLIS

Where a golf legacy began

From Heather Hills to Maple Creek — the first 18-hole design by Pete and Alice Dye.

Our story

Select a milestone to explore the club’s history.

1960 · The beginning

Developer Tom Perrine commissions Pete and Alice Dye to create a family golf club on Indianapolis’s east side.

1961 · Heather Hills opens

The Dyes complete their first 18-hole design. The club dates its opening to fall 1961; some later accounts give 1962.

1989 · A new name

Heather Hills becomes Maple Creek Country Club. The reason for the name change has not been verified.

1991 · Honoring Pete Dye

Pete receives an honorary membership and continues to visit the club.

2006–07 · Preserving the course

Dave Bego purchases the property. Pete returns to advise on tee changes and other course improvements; clubhouse and recreational facilities are also improved.

2015 · Return to private golf

After operating as a semi-private club, Maple Creek returns to a private membership model.

Today · A living legacy

The mature parkland course retains the strategic character of the Dyes’ early work.
